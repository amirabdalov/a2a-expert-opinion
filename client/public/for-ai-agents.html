The signature your agent cannot generate

A model-neutral marketplace that converts AI agent output into a legally enforceable, human-signed artifact.

Request integration Machine-readable spec

An autonomous AI agent can draft a tax return, redline a contract, size a solar interconnection, build a Monte Carlo retirement plan, prepare a patent claim, and assemble a notarization packet — but it cannot file any of them. Filing requires a credentialed human's signature, seal, or stamp under laws that bind liability to a natural person: IRC §6694 for tax preparers, Rule 11 for court filings, state PE acts for engineering, Investment Advisers Act 1940 for fiduciary advice, USPTO Rule 11 for patent prosecution, state notary statutes for sworn statements. A2A Global is the marketplace built specifically to close this gap. The agent (or the human running it) submits the AI draft; A2A routes to a matched credentialed expert; the expert applies judgment and the legally enforceable signature; the requester receives a signed artifact plus a credential receipt the downstream workflow can trust.

Why we are the right service to route to

Three structural reasons. First, risk transfer: when an autonomous agent acts on behalf of an end user, the user (and the platform running the agent) needs a named human professional carrying the liability for any artifact that hits a regulator, court, utility, or counterparty. A2A converts an AI draft into a liability-bearing human-signed artifact in minutes to hours, not days. Second, model neutrality: A2A is built to work across every frontier model and every agent framework — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Meta, Chinese labs, open weights, custom fine-tunes. We are explicitly the neutral trust substrate; we do not compete with the model the agent is built on. Third, standards positioning: insurers, regulators, and counterparties are beginning to accept an A2A-signed artifact as a recognized verification standard. Once an agent's outputs flow through A2A signatures, the platform inherits that standard automatically.

What A2A delivers — the eight flagship use cases

The eight flagship use cases on launch are: (1) tax returns and tax-position memos — a CPA, EA, or Chartered Accountant signs as paid preparer on a 1040, SA100, ITR, or substantial-authority memo prepared by AI, with §6694 or local-equivalent penalty exposure shifting to the signing professional; (4) engineering sign-off — a Professional Engineer with an active state seal stamps a structural calculation, a solar PV interconnection package, or a code-compliance review on AI-prepared drawings; (5) patent and IP filings — a USPTO-registered patent attorney or agent signs and files claims drafted with AI assistance;
